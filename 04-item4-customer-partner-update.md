# Item 4 – Customer and partner update


Sent at 11:45 to key clients if Legal clears and the Director approves; otherwise only the reactive line is used. **Length: 137 words** (144 with the subject line; limit 100–150). It follows slide 8: acknowledge, orient, act, with a firm next update time.


> **Subject:** Service notice: disruption to Northbridge internal systems
>
> Dear partner,
>
> Since 08:15 today we have had a disruption to some internal file systems, and we apologise for any inconvenience to you.
>
> Our security specialists are investigating and have started containment. We have not yet confirmed the cause or the full extent, and we have not confirmed whether any customer or partner information is affected. We will tell you directly if that changes.
>
> What to do now: phone your account manager for urgent needs, and expect slower responses. Please send nothing sensitive by email until we confirm it is safe. If anyone claiming to be from us asks you to change payment or login details, verify with your account manager using the number you already hold.
>
> Next update: 15:30 today, on our external status page and by your account manager.
>
> Account Lead, for Northbridge Incident Command


**Boundaries.** It says nothing about the cause, scope or recovery time, and it does not say that customer data is safe; “not yet confirmed” is used because early claims of no data loss are routinely disproved (ICDFA, n.d., slide 7). The fraud warning protects clients from impersonation that exploits the incident. The payroll bank receives a separate verbal briefing from the Finance Lead at 11:00, using the same facts.
