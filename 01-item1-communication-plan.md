# Item 1 – One-page incident communication plan


**Objective.** Within the 08:30–12:30 window, establish credibility, stop information leakage and give every stakeholder group what it needs to act, while stating only what is verified (ICDFA, n.d., slides 3 and 18). **Posture.** Crisis communication is declared at 08:30: duration has no ETA, payroll is at risk and the screenshot is external (slide 13).


| **Role (owner of)** | **Responsibility in this plan** |
|---|---|
| **Incident Commander** | Owns priorities, decision cadence and final approval of every release. |
| **Communications Lead (me)** | Drafts, sequences, runs the time-stamped ledger, tracks acknowledgements and the update clock. |
| **Technical Lead / Legal** | Validate facts (10 min) / clear regulatory, privilege and contractual risk (15 min). |
| **People, Finance, Account Leads** | Send to staff / own payroll and bank contact / own customer contact. |
| **Director (spokesperson)** | Only external voice. All other staff say: “We are investigating; the Director’s office will update at 15:30.” |


*Wave 1, 08:30–12:30: first three communications (C1–C3) plus two follow-ups (C4–C5).*


| **#** | **Time** | **Message and audience** | **Purpose** | **Sender / approver** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 08:45 | Incident command alignment: IC, Director, Technical, Legal, People, Finance, Account Leads (phone bridge, 15 min) | One source of truth; named voices; disclosure rules; approve employee text | IC / Director |
| C2 | 09:15 | Employee holding statement: all staff by SMS and manager cascade (Item 3) | Close the information vacuum; neutralise screenshot; operating rules; payroll checkpoint | People Lead / IC, Legal |
| C3 | 10:30 | Leadership briefing (Item 5), then payroll bank call at 11:00 by Finance Lead | Decision thresholds, payroll gates, containment risk, next review | IC, Finance / Director |
| C4 | 11:45 | Customer and partner update to key clients (Item 4), if Legal clears | Continuity assurance, risk disclosure, anti-fraud warning | Account Lead / Director, Legal |
| C5 | 12:00 | Employee update 2, sent even if nothing has changed | Keep the promised time; reset next checkpoint 15:00 | People Lead / IC |


**Order logic.** Voices are aligned first, because no message may leave before a single source of truth exists. Employees come second: they are closest, most numerous and the origin of the leak, and a late update lets rumour fill the gap (slide 15). Leaders and the bank come third so that payroll decisions are taken before customers hear anything (slide 14).


**Release workflow (target 30 min).** Draft (Comms) → fact check (Technical) → legal check (Legal) → approve (IC) → send by out-of-band channel (Item 6) → log time, sender and acknowledgements. Raw working notes stay separate from approved text (slide 6). A pre-approved reactive line covers inbound questions: “We are aware of a disruption to some of our systems and are investigating with specialists. The next update is at 15:30.”


**Cadence.** Staff: 09:15, 12:00, 15:00, 17:30, then 08:30 and 16:00 daily. Leadership: 10:30, 12:30, 16:30, then 08:00 and 17:00 daily. Clients: 11:45, 15:30, then 10:00 daily. Bank: 11:00 Monday, then 10:00 Tuesday and Wednesday and 09:00 Thursday. Command sync: 08:45, 10:15, 12:15, 15:45. Every checkpoint is kept even with “no change”, because a broken timeline damages trust faster than the outage (slide 7).


**Never in any message:** attacker or cause; “ransomware” confirmed; “no evidence of data loss”; a recovery time; “payroll is safe”; screenshot content; blame of a vendor. **Escalate to the Board Chair** on: likely personal-data access, media or regulator contact, payroll gate failure, a genuine screenshot, or any message not acknowledged within 90 minutes. **Stand down** only when services, payroll and trust are restored and the IC signs off (slide 19).
