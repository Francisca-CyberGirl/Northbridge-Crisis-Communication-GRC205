# Item 2 – Stakeholder map


Eight groups are mapped, including the five required cohorts. Tier 1 groups must hear from us at or before the first release; Tier 2 groups inside the window; Tier 3 groups only on a trigger. Owners follow the pre-assigned model on slide 4, which prevents conflicting statements in early chaos. Needs follow slide 9.


*Table 3. Stakeholder matrix (owner = message owner; all releases approved by the Incident Commander).*


| **Group and tier** | **What they need** | **What we ask of them** | **Owner** | **First contact and cadence** | **Must not be told** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Direct workforce** (about 180). Tier 1 | Operating rules; payroll status without speculation; guidance on the screenshot; a way to keep working | Follow rules; do not forward the image; report sightings and suspicious messages; reply “1” to confirm receipt | People Lead | 09:15, then 12:00, 15:00, 17:30; from Tuesday 08:30 and 16:00 | Cause; “ransomware”; “payroll is safe”; image content |
| **2. Executive leadership and Board.** Tier 1 | Fiduciary exposure (Item 5); legal liability; decision triggers; options and dependencies | Decisions D1–D6; protect the single voice; authorise fallback and specialists | Incident Commander | 10:30, then 12:30, 16:30; Tuesday 08:00 and 17:00 | Any unverified scope or recovery estimate |
| **3. Managers and department heads.** Tier 1 | Short talking points; temporary working methods; who to call | Cascade the message; confirm each team member; hold speculation | People Lead | 09:15 with staff; call-tree check 10:15; sync 10:15 | Anything not in the approved text |
| **4. Regulators and legal counsel.** Tier 1 (counsel); Tier 3 (regulator) | Precise, privileged, auditable record; scope when known; mitigation actions | Legal advice on disclosure, privilege and the regulator clock (A7); approve every external text | Legal / Compliance | Counsel in C1 at 08:45; regulator only on Legal’s decision; assessment check 12:30 and Tuesday 09:00 | “No personal data affected”; “breach” before Legal characterises it |
| **5. | **6. Key customers and B2B clients.** Tier 2 | Service impact; secure workaround; next scheduled update; third-party risk disclosure | Use phone for urgent needs; avoid sensitive email; verify unusual requests by call-back | Account Lead | 11:45, then 15:30; 10:00 daily | “Your data is safe”; cause; recovery time |
